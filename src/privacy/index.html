Tune Up

Privacy Policy

What this site hears from you and records about you. (tl;dr: not much)

Signed Out

You can play any published tune while signed out, and all of the data about your progress on a tune—that is, the pitches you've written, your feedback from checks, how many checks you made, how much time you spent, and whether you activated assist mode—is kept in your own browser's local storage. None of this data is ever stored by the site.

In particular, none of your data while signed out ever reaches a database, so nothing you do ever contributes to any statistic publicly displayed on this site. The only data the server receives is the checks that you request (and this data is never recorded).

Signing In: Google Access

When you sign in with Google, this site receives the following three things:

  • Your account's machine-readable ID (a meaningless string of numbers).
  • Your email address.
  • Confirmation that Google has verified your email address.

In particular, this site never receives or stores any information about your Google profile. Although this site does store your email address, this site only uses your email address for distinguishing between users in the database in a human-readable way. This site will never use it to contact you and will never disclose it to third parties.

Signing In: Account Data

Every account holds the following data, recorded in this site's database:

  • Your username. This site automatically generates a username for your account once it is created. You may change your username at any time. Your username is displayed beside every tune you publish (unless you choose to be shown as Anonymous)—aside from that, your username is kept to yourself.
  • Your tunes. Unpublished tunes are kept to yourself and never publicly accessible. Published tunes are public: their title, subtitle, instructions, difficulty, and associated YouTube video can be seen by anyone. You may unpublish a published tune at any time; doing so will permanently delete all progress, upvotes, and difficulty suggestions on that tune, including your own.
  • Your progress. This site stores your account's progress on public tunes and private drafts in a database. No other users can ever see your progress.
  • Settings. There are only two: whether your username is shown as Anonymous, and whether your activity on public tunes counts towards any public statistics.

If you sign in to an account for the first time on a browser that has progress stored in its local storage, the site will give you the option to merge your browser's progress into your account.

Cookies

This site uses two cookies.

  • session. This is a random token that lasts 30 days, is extended as you keep visiting the site, and is deleted when you sign out. The database only ever stores a hash of this token.
  • signin. This token exists for up to ten minutes between when you are directed to sign in with Google and when you return to this site.

There are no analytics cookies and no advertising.

YouTube

Tunes are transcribed from YouTube videos. This site embeds YouTube's own player onto the page, and the list of tunes displays thumbnails fetched from YouTube. All requests to YouTube are seen as ordinary visits to a YouTube page and operate under Google's privacy policy. This site sends nothing to YouTube beyond what the YouTube player and thumbnail-retrieval need.

Public Statistics

Each tune displays a difficulty estimate, the number of upvotes it has received, median completion times, and the number of signed-in users (besides the author) who have completed it. All of these statistics are aggregate, and no individual accounts are explicitly named in any statistics. Refer to the About page for further details on how difficulty estimates and median completion times are decided.

The account page has a setting “Contribute to public stats?” that, when toggled off, removes all data concerning your upvote history, your difficulty ratings, and your completions of public tunes from the computations of public stats. Note, however, that this data still remains in the database; if you toggle this setting back on, all of this data will be factored back into public stats.

What can the site owner see?

The person who runs this site (hi! 👋) can read all of the data recorded in its database. (Said person will not do so for any reason other than to keep the site functioning.) No data is ever sold or shared with anyone else.

The database lives on Cloudflare's infrastructure and consequently under Cloudflare's privacy policy. Every request to this site is served through Cloudflare as well, which sees your IP address the same way any web host would; notably, this site itself never reads, logs, or stores your IP address.

Account Deletion

Deleting your account deletes all data associated with it. This includes every tune you've ever published (in addition to other users' progress, upvotes, and difficulty suggestions on them), your drafts, your progress on public tunes and private drafts, your upvotes and difficulty suggestions on public tunes, your settings, your username, your email, and your Google account identifier. Account deletion cannot be undone.
