Isolate tricky sections and listen closer

Need to take a closer listen to a particularly tricky section? You can loop any section of the music you'd like by simply selecting a range of notes straight off the staff.

Keep listening until you've got the music in your head, then get to transcribing!

Check your work, receive feedback, and try again

Once you've worked out a pitch for every note, check your work against an answer key. Receive feedback on what you got right, and learn which notes still need fixing.

No worries if you made a few mistakes! Check your work as many times as you need until you've got everything just right.

Challenge yourself with a wide variety of tunes

Anyone can publish a tune they've made for anyone else to challenge themselves with.

Pick out just the right tunes to train your ear with by searching for tunes that match your ideal spiciness level.

Train your ear on tunes spanning a variety of artists and genres—whether it's a track you've had on repeat for years, or a style you're hearing for the very first time.
